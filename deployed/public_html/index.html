Long-haul tractor 65 T GCM · 6×4

Distance work. A defined driveline.

The G7H pairs a 12.419-litre MC13.48 with a ZF 12-speed AMT: 480 hp and 2300 Nm.

65 T GCM refers to the gross combination mass, not the cargo you can load. Discuss the trailer, route and axle loads before choosing the combination.

Bring these to the conversation

  • Trailer type and loaded combination mass
  • Route gradients and distance between stops
  • Sleeper requirements and driver changeovers
